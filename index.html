FAQ

Questions

Does the creator need to sign up before I launch?

No. Fees collect in the @handle's vault from the first trade. When the creator verifies, they receive everything owed at once.

How does a creator prove the account is theirs?

They get a one-time code on the Claim page and paste it into their TikTok bio. FYPaid reads the public profile once, then they can delete the code.

What if the creator never claims?

Unclaimed fees return to the launcher's wallet after 60 days.

Is this trustless?

No. Fees route through the operator's vault and are paid out by script. Only launch what you're comfortable trusting a custodian with.

Is FYPaid part of TikTok?

No. FYPaid is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok. A coin naming a creator doesn't mean they endorse it until they verify.
